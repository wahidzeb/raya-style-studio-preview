About Raya Style Studio

Raya Style Studio is a cheerful Hari Raya dress-up game. Help Aisyah and her little brother Amir get ready for Raya morning, the open house and the big family photo.

Choose a style for each of them: baju kurung, kebaya labuh and a matching tudung for Aisyah, and baju Melayu with a sampin, songkok or tengkolok for Amir. Pick colours and patterns like songket, batik, pucuk rebung and floral, then add a ketupat, a pelita or a little kitten and choose a Raya backdrop. Family members send Raya requests with three wishes. Meet the wishes, take the photo and earn stars to open new styles, colours, patterns and places. In the Free Studio you can style anything you like and keep your favourite photos in the album.

3 quick tips

  1. Read the three wishes first. Every wish you meet gives one star, and you can retry a request for a better score.
  2. Sedondon means the whole family wears the same colour. Dress Aisyah and Amir in one colour to meet those wishes.
  3. Tap Surprise in the Free Studio for a fresh look, then change one piece at a time.

How to play

👆 Tap Aisyah or Amir to choose who to dress.

👘 Pick a tab, like Baju or Sampin, then tap a colour or a pattern.

💌 In Raya Requests, your family asks for a look. Each wish you match gives a star ⭐. Tap the wish card at the top to see the list.

📸 Tap Photo when you are happy. Stars open new styles, colours, patterns and places.

🎨 In the Free Studio you can dress them any way you like and keep up to 12 photos in your album.
